# Tesserae Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Tesserae is a desktop word game. It is built to run entirely on your own computer.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Tesserae:

- does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information;
- has no accounts, no sign-in, and no tracking;
- contains no advertising and no analytics;
- does not read your files, contacts, location, or camera.

## Network use

Tesserae only uses your network connection for its optional local multiplayer feature (playing against other Tesserae players on a network you choose, such as your home network) and to fetch nothing else. Game data stays between the players on that network. It is never sent to us, because there is no "us" server — the app has no backend.

## Microsoft Store

If you obtain Tesserae through the Microsoft Store, Microsoft handles all purchase and billing data under Microsoft's own [privacy statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement). We never see your payment details or personal information.
